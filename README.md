# 💸 ExpenseTracker
### Track Smarter. Spend Better. Stay in Control.

> **Small expenses add up. Smart tracking makes every rupee count.**

ExpenseTracker is a modern, responsive, and interactive expense management web application built using **HTML5, Tailwind CSS, and JavaScript**.

Designed with simplicity and usability in mind, it helps users organize daily expenses, monitor monthly budgets, and understand spending patterns through a clean and intuitive dashboard.

## 🎯 The Idea Behind ExpenseTracker

Ever wondered where your monthly budget disappears?

A coffee here, a movie there, a quick shopping trip — individually, these expenses seem small, but together they affect our savings.

**ExpenseTracker was built to solve this everyday challenge.**

The goal is simple:

**Make personal expense management easy, visual, and accessible.**

No complicated dashboards. No unnecessary features. Just a straightforward way to understand your spending.

---

## ✨ Features That Make a Difference

### 📊 1. Smart Financial Dashboard
- View your monthly budget instantly.
- Monitor your total expenses.
- Track your remaining balance.
- Automatically update financial summaries.

### ➕ 2. Effortless Expense Management
- Add expenses with a name and amount.
- Assign categories to transactions.
- Delete individual expenses.
- Clear the entire expense list.

### 📈 3. Visual Budget Monitoring
- Dynamic progress bar showing budget utilization.
- Percentage-based spending indicator.
- Helpful budget status messages.
- Instant updates whenever expenses change.

### 🗂️ 4. Organized Expense Categories
Keep your spending organized through categories such as:

- 🍔 Food
- ✈️ Travel
- 🎬 Entertainment
- 🛍️ Shopping
- 📦 Other Expenses

### 📱 5. Responsive & Minimal Interface
- Clean dashboard layout.
- Mobile-friendly design.
- Simple navigation.
- Easy-to-understand financial information.


### Step 2: Navigate to the Project

```bash

```

Open `index.html` in your browser.
